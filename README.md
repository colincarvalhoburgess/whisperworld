# Whisperworld

**A voice-navigated spatial storytelling platform combining AI voice, immersive audio and interactive narrative.**

**[Launch the working prototype →](https://audioworld.app/world)**

## Overview

Whisperworld explores a simple question:

> **What happens when listening becomes participation?**

Rather than navigating an audio story through a conventional screen interface, listeners enter a spatial sound world, orient themselves through audio, speak to interact and move through branching stories that can remember their progress.

I'm developing Whisperworld as both an interactive storytelling experience and a repeatable production system for creating further audio worlds.

## What I've built

The current working prototype and Creator Studio bring together:

- ElevenLabs-generated character voices
- Recorded dialogue and performance
- Voice-controlled interaction
- Spatial / HRTF audio
- Branching narrative
- Persistent story progress
- Creative sound design
- An authoring workflow for building additional worlds

## ElevenLabs and generative voice

ElevenLabs is part of the production workflow rather than a standalone demonstration.

I've used it to create and cast character voices, explore different vocal qualities and performances, and generate dialogue that sits alongside recorded speech, spatial audio and creative sound design. I've also explored ElevenLabs generative tools as part of building the wider sound world.

What interests me particularly is what happens **after the first generation**.

I apply professional audio and post-production judgement to:

- Voice casting and timbre
- Pacing and intelligibility
- Emotional intent and performance consistency
- Dialogue editing
- Identifying artefacts or performances that don't work
- Regeneration and iterative refinement
- Integration with recorded performances
- Sound design and spatial positioning
- Balancing speech with the wider soundscape
- Final quality control

That process draws on more than 20 years of professional audio work in Pro Tools, including dialogue editing, recording, mixing, sound design and post-production for organisations including the BBC and Audible/Amazon.

## Iterative development

Whisperworld has been developed in working increments rather than as a static concept.

Each iteration tests whether the experience remains coherent for the listener: whether they can orient themselves through sound, whether voice interaction moves the story forward reliably, whether generated and recorded performances sit naturally together, and whether pacing, intelligibility and spatial positioning support rather than distract from the story.

The development loop is deliberately similar to an audio post-production process:

**Build → Listen → Diagnose → Edit / Regenerate → Integrate → Test again**

This has meant refining dialogue timing, voice consistency, performance, spatial placement, sound design and the balance between speech and the wider soundscape.

## How it works

At a high level, the experience connects:

**Listener → Voice interaction → Story logic → Character / dialogue response → Spatial audio → Listener**

Behind the experience, the Creator Studio is intended to make that system repeatable rather than tying it to a single demonstration.

## What success looks like

At this stage, success isn't a revenue or audience number. The first proof point was whether the idea could actually work as an experience.

The working prototype demonstrates that a listener can enter an audio world, navigate and interact using their voice, encounter AI-generated characters and progress through branching, persistent stories.

Building the Creator Studio alongside the listener experience tests a second proposition: that Whisperworld can become a production tool for creating further stories and worlds, rather than remaining a one-off prototype.

The goal isn't simply to prove that the technology can generate individual components, but to iterate until **voice, dialogue, sound design, spatial audio and interactive storytelling work together as a coherent produced experience**.
